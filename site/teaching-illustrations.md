## ML Foundations image prompt

> Use case: scientific-educational. Original wide editorial illustration for a university Machine Learning Lab lesson about forecasting morning rental demand. A street-level bike-share dock on a Washington DC neighborhood street, several muted red rental bicycles docked in a row, one helmeted commuter unlocking a bike just before the morning commute. Period-neutral street details, soft morning light, ivory sky, forest teal greens, rust red bikes, believable human proportions and realistic bicycle geometry. Sophisticated gouache and fine ink on lightly textured paper, calm and uncluttered. Frame the bikes and commuter so they read in a landscape course card and a 4:3 inset. No logos, lettering, numbers, charts, watermarks, or captions. This is an illustration, not a depiction of any particular historical observation.
