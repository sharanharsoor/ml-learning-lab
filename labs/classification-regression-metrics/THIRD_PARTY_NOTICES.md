# Third-party data sources

The app and notebooks are distributed under the repository’s MIT license. The UCI records and selected data derived from them retain their dataset licenses.

- **Spambase.** Hopkins, M., Reeber, E., Forman, G., and Suermondt, J. (1999). *Spambase*. UCI Machine Learning Repository. The lab exports selected row IDs, rounded model scores, and observed labels. [Dataset and citation](https://doi.org/10.24432/C53G6X) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Bike Sharing.** Fanaee-T, H. (2013). *Bike Sharing*. UCI Machine Learning Repository. The source records Capital Bikeshare trips aggregated by hour. The lab exports selected dates and counts plus forecasts and test metrics calculated in this repository. [Dataset and citation](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5W894) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
