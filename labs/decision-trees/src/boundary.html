This sandbox uses 21 different parcels. The two inputs are weight X in kilograms and scan score Z. The scanner scores its first image of the shipping label from 0 to 10. A higher value warns of a harder-to-read label, for example because the image looks smudged.

The sandbox uses invented measurements and routes so we can practice drawing cuts. Parcel 8 weighed 3.6 kg and went to Auto with a scan score of 9.3. The marker shows the example staff decision. Shading shows the model’s prediction.

Click or drag to preview a cut. The dashed line snaps to a midpoint between observed values in the selected region. Apply the cut to ask whether the measurement is at most that cutoff. Select a child region to make the next cut there.

Where do the two worked cutoffs come from?

The worked example starts at 5 kg, halfway between 4 and 6 kg, the two observed weights on either side of the first cut. Among parcels heavier than 5 kg, the nearest observed scan scores on either side of the second cut are 4 and 6. The example uses their midpoint, 5, to divide that region.
