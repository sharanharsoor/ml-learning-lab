## Bootstrap sampling

[Source image](scene-bootstrap.jpg)

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: wide classroom slide art. In a modern parcel sorting center, show a warm editorial illustration with confident, simple shapes, dark evergreen and terracotta accents, a pale cream wall, clean consistent outlines, soft shadows, and adult staff in high-visibility vests. Key subjects sit in the central 60 percent of the image height for a short projector-friendly crop. Wide horizontal 16:9. No writing, numbers, arrows, logos, watermarks, or signage. Show one warehouse worker on the left lifting a single yellow parcel-record ticket from a visibly full teal tray. The same worker is also placing a plain duplicate record slip into a separate clay sample tray on the right, with matching yellow cards visible among the added slips, a few differently colored cards nearby. The original tray and sample tray must look like separate work surfaces. The scene teaches drawing with replacement and recording repeated draws. Show the selected ticket and hands clearly, with few objects.
