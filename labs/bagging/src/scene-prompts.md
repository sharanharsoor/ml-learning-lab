# Bagging lesson illustrations

Original editorial artwork generated with ImageGen for the Spambase lesson. A warm cream, teal, and terracotta palette connects the five sections. The artwork illustrates the teaching scenario. It contains no original email text or model calculations.

- [Inbox](email-desk.jpg) shows an incoming message and the two possible folders.
- [Bootstrap sampling](scene-bootstrap.jpg) shows records being drawn into a separate tray and the same design appearing twice.
- [Forest prediction](scene-forest.jpg) shows the same email going to three different trees.
- [Rows and features](scene-diversity.jpg) contrasts a stack of email records with individual feature tokens.
- [Out of bag](scene-oob.jpg) shows an email left outside a training tray.

The diagrams and plots in the lesson render separately from the artwork. Their numbers come from the fitted examples.
